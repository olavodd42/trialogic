OFFICIAL TITLE: TriaLogic Internal Protocol Registry
BASED ON: Sepsis-3, NEWS2 (RCP), MEWS, qSOFA, SIRS
SCOPE: Conceptual risk interpretation and physiological grounding only.
NOTE: This document does not provide diagnostic or therapeutic recommendations.

SECTION 1: AGGREGATE SCORE INTERPRETATION
1.1 NEWS2 Total Score Thresholds
   - A total NEWS2 score of 0–4 indicates Low Clinical Risk.
   - A score of 3 in any single parameter, regardless of total score, indicates Low–Medium Risk, requiring ward-level clinical response.
   - A total NEWS2 score of 5–6 indicates Medium Clinical Risk, requiring urgent clinical response.
   - A total NEWS2 score of 7 or more indicates High Clinical Risk, requiring emergency response.

1.2 MEWS Total Score Thresholds
   - A total MEWS score of 0–2 is considered physiological / low risk.
   - A total MEWS score of 3–4 requires increased monitoring and ward review.
   - A total MEWS score of 5 or more indicates critical deterioration, warranting Rapid Response Team (RRT/MET) activation.

SECTION 2: NORMAL PHYSIOLOGICAL RANGES (BASELINE)
(Values corresponding to NEWS2 score = 0)

   - Heart Rate: 51–90 beats per minute
   - Respiratory Rate: 12–20 breaths per minute
   - Systolic BP: 111–219 mmHg
   - Oxygen Saturation: ≥96% on room air (NEWS2 Scale 1)
   - Temperature: 36.1–38.0 °C

SECTION 3: HEART RATE CRITERIA (TACHYCARDIA/BRADYCARDIA)

3.1 SIRS Heart Rate Criteria: 
   - Heart Rate > 90 beats/min.

SECTION 4: HYPOTENSION AND SHOCK CRITERIA
4.1 Sepsis-3 Hypotension Criterion
   - Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) <65 mmHg

4.2 Septic Shock Clinical Definition (Sepsis-3)
Septic shock is defined as a subset of sepsis with:
   - Persisting hypotension requiring vasopressors to maintain MAP ≥65 mmHg
   AND
   - Serum lactate >2 mmol/L (18 mg/dL)
   despite adequate volume resuscitation.

4.3 qSOFA Hypotension Criterion
   - Systolic Blood Pressure ≤100 mmHg

SECTION 5: TACHYPNEA AND RESPIRATORY CRITERIA
5.1 SIRS Respiratory Criteria
   - Respiratory Rate >20 breaths per minute
   OR
   - PaCO₂ <32 mmHg

2. qSOFA Respiratory Criterion
   - Respiratory Rate ≥22 breaths per minute

SECTION 6: ALTERED MENTAL STATUS

6.1 qSOFA Neurological Criterion
   - Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) <15

SECTION 7: FEVER AND SEPSIS-RELATED CRITERIA
8.1 SIRS Temperature Criterion
   - Temperature >38.0 °C
   OR
   - Temperature <36.0 °C

8.2 Sepsis Definition (Sepsis-3)
Sepsis is defined as life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection, operationalized as:
   - an acute increase in SOFA score of ≥2 points from baseline,
   in the presence of suspected or confirmed infection.

FINAL NOTE ON INTENDED USE

This registry is intended for:
   - Physiological risk interpretation
   - Score grounding and explanation
   - Safety-oriented reasoning and escalation justification

It is not intended for:
   - Diagnostic determination
   - Treatment selection
   - Protocolized clinical decision-making

When uncertainty exists, the system must default to silence rather than extrapolation.
