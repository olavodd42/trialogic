SOURCE: GLOBAL CLINICAL DEFINITIONS (GOLD STANDARD CHEAT SHEET)
OFFICIAL TITLE: TriaLogic Internal Protocol Registry (Based on Sepsis-3/NEWS2)

SECTION: AGGREGATE SCORE INTERPRETATION (CRITICAL)
1. NEWS2 Total Score Thresholds:
   - A total NEWS2 score of 0-4 indicates Low Clinical Risk.
   - A total NEWS2 score of 3 in any single parameter indicates Low-Medium Risk (Ward Response).
   - A total NEWS2 score of 5-6 indicates Medium Clinical Risk (Urgent Response).
   - A total NEWS2 score of 7 or more indicates High Clinical Risk (Emergency Response).

2. MEWS Total Score Thresholds:
   - A total MEWS score of 0-2 is considered physiological.
   - A total MEWS score of 3-4 requires increased monitoring (Ward Review).
   - A total MEWS score of 5 or more is critical (Call RRT/MET).

SECTION: NORMAL PHYSIOLOGICAL RANGES (BASELINE)
1. Heart Rate: 51-90 bpm is considered normal (Score 0).
2. Respiratory Rate: 12-20 breaths/min is considered normal (Score 0).
3. Systolic BP: 111-219 mmHg is considered normal (Score 0).
4. Oxygen Saturation: >= 96% on air is considered normal (Score 0).
5. Temperature: 36.1-38.0 C is considered normal (Score 0).

SECTION: HEART RATE CRITERIA (TACHYCARDIA/BRADYCARDIA)
1. SIRS Heart Rate Criteria: Heart Rate > 90 beats/min.
2. NEWS2 Heart Rate Scoring:
   - Heart Rate <= 40 scores +3 points (Severe Bradycardia).
   - Heart Rate 41-50 scores +1 point.
   - Heart Rate 91-110 scores +1 point.
   - Heart Rate 111-130 scores +2 points.
   - Heart Rate >= 131 scores +3 points (Severe Tachycardia).

SECTION: HYPOTENSION AND SHOCK CRITERIA
1. Sepsis-3 Definition of Hypotension: Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) < 65 mmHg.
2. Septic Shock Clinical Criteria: Persisting hypotension requiring vasopressors to maintain MAP >= 65 mmHg AND Serum Lactate level > 2 mmol/L (18 mg/dL) despite adequate volume resuscitation.
3. NEWS2 Hypotension Scoring (Systolic BP): 
   - Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) <= 90 mmHg scores +3 points (High Risk).
   - Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) between 91-100 mmHg scores +2 points.
   - Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) between 101-110 mmHg scores +1 point.
4. qSOFA Hypotension Criteria: Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) <= 100 mmHg.

SECTION: TACHYPNEA AND RESPIRATORY CRITERIA
1. SIRS Respiratory Criteria: Respiratory Rate > 20 breaths/min OR PaCO2 < 32 mmHg.
2. qSOFA Respiratory Criteria: Respiratory Rate >= 22 breaths/min.
3. NEWS2 Respiratory Scoring:
   - Respiratory Rate <= 8 scores +3 points.
   - Respiratory Rate 9-11 scores +1 point.
   - Respiratory Rate 21-24 scores +2 points.
   - Respiratory Rate >= 25 scores +3 points.

SECTION: OXYGENATION AND SATURATION CRITERIA
1. NEWS2 Oxygen Saturation Scoring (Scale 1):
   - SpO2 <= 91% scores +3 points (Critical Hypoxia).
   - SpO2 92-93% scores +2 points.
   - SpO2 94-95% scores +1 point.
2. NEWS2 Supplemental Oxygen:
   - Any use of Supplemental Oxygen (Yes) scores +2 points.

SECTION: ALTERED MENTAL STATUS
1. qSOFA GCS Criteria: Glasgow Coma Scale < 15.
2. NEWS2 Consciousness: Any status other than "Alert" (e.g., Voice, Pain, Unresponsive or Confusion) scores +3 points.

SECTION: FEVER AND SEPSIS SIGNS
1. SIRS Temperature Criteria: Temperature > 38.0 C or < 36.0 C.
2. NEWS2 Temperature Scoring:
   - Temperature <= 35.0 scores +3 points.
   - Temperature 35.1-36.0 scores +1 point.
   - Temperature 38.1-39.0 scores +1 point.
   - Temperature >= 39.1 scores +2 points.
3. Sepsis Definition (Sepsis-3): Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection (SOFA score change >= 2).
